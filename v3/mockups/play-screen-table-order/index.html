The play screen in table order

Today your Reserve sits above your Field, so fielding a die sends it down the screen. This puts your side in the order it has on a table: Reserve nearest you, Field ahead of it, your attack zone ahead of that, then their attack zone and their Field. Screenshots are the game today; the others are a live mock of the proposal at 402×874. The yellow dashed tags are notes, not part of the design.

What moved

  1. Your Field moved from the bottom of the screen to the middle, directly under their Field.
  2. The attack zones are a thin seam between the Fields outside the Attack step. During it they open into one strip joined to both Fields: their zone on top, yours below, lined up lane for lane.
  3. A lane is still one box holding the attacker and its blocker, so the fight reads as a unit; the box is cream-white, belonging to neither side.
  4. Reserve and the buy cards moved below your Field, nearest your thumb.
  5. The Global abilities moved from between the two panels to under the buy cards.
  6. Your nameplate and piles moved to the bottom, mirroring the opponent's at the top.
