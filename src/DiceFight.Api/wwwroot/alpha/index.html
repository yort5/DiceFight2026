DiceFight v3 — playable prototype

Instinct Clash

Pass-and-play, two players, one device. No card abilities on any individual creature yet, on purpose — the only "ability" in the game is each Champion's single passive team-wide effect, so today's reactions stay on the system rather than any one card's power level.

How to play (read once)
Each player picks one Champion. A Champion has no die of its own — it's a passive effect that's always on for your whole team, nothing more. Your actual pool is 1 Character die (an individual creature with its own printed fielding and purchase cost, the way a real Dice Masters character works) plus 4 Tardigrade dice — your Champion's basic, generic troop, always free to field, same stat line for every energy type, only the energy color differs. You can buy up to 3 more copies of your Character mid-game. 20 life each.
  • Draw & Roll — recycle your discard back into your bag, draw 4 dice, roll them. Each die may be rerolled once.
  • Main — Field a die showing a creature face (Tardigrades are free; your Character costs energy, any type), or Purchase another Character copy (only matching-type or Wild energy pays purchase cost). Do this as many times as you can afford.
  • Attack — pick any of your fielded creatures to attack. Defender assigns blockers 1-for-1. Unblocked attackers hit the opponent's life directly; blocked pairs trade damage, and anyone whose defense drops to 0 is KO'd to the discard.
Lose by hitting 0 life, or by having zero dice left to draw. The Tardigrade die's six faces: two L1 (weak body, 2 energy), two L2 (better body, 1 energy), one Bulwark (best body, no energy), one Surge (no body, 2 Wild energy).
First-pass prototype — not balanced, not final art. Specifically want reactions on: does each Champion's passive feel worth building around, does the uneven Tardigrade die create real decisions, and does fielding-vs-purchasing your one Character feel meaningful.
